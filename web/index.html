Campus Accessibility Route Finder

TCET 2nd floor modelled as an undirected weighted graph. Routes are filtered for stairs, narrow passages, lifts, and blocked corridors.

Find a route

Click a room on the map to set start, then another for destination. Click a path to block or unblock it.
